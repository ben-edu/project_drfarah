## 2. What Google charges for — avoid a false 'conversion fee' fix

For these Search Manual CPC campaigns, ad clicks are billable, **not extra conversion events**, and the conversion-measurement feature is free. Cost/conv = already-incurred ad spend ÷ counted conversions. Example: $302.30/8 ≈ **$37.79 per counted call conversion**, NOT $37.79 on top of the clicks and NOT cost per completed patient. Conversion reporting can inform keyword, creative and lead-quality decisions; deleting the tracking does not remove click charges and makes attribution less observable. Google *can* use chosen Primary goals to optimize future conversion-based Smart Bidding, but current manual CPC with Enhanced CPC false is not automatically optimizing bids to those calls. Keep bidding mode controlled until verified downstream outcomes justify a change.

Current `Calls from ads` action is ENABLED, `primary_for_goal=true`, `MANY_PER_CLICK` ('Every'); three Google-hosted Local actions are also marked Primary at their own goal/action levels but recorded as **All Conversions only** for the sampled Search campaign. Breakdown of 37 All Conversions: 8 Calls from ads + 12 Directions + 15 Other engagements + 2 Website visits. 16 reported phone_calls versus 8 counted calls does not establish 16 or 8 patients: call conversion uses a *configured minimum call duration* and counting semantics. Verify duration, Count One vs Every, per-campaign selected goal, action value and attribution; choose **One per ad interaction** if clinic wants unique leads, not a count of every repeated call. Preserve call history and do not assume changing Count retroactively changes earlier rows. Do not make number clicks, directions, page views or booking starts a Primary patient conversion.

**Website source inspection:** `frontend/analytics.js` uses GA4 `G-D58FTMF05S` only on six public informational nonclinical production pages under consent guard; no automatic ad-conversion event or Google marketing tag on health-service/booking/registration pages. `frontend/booking.js` and first-party attribution layer already preserve source/UTM and permitted click identifiers through a booking request. `Calls from ads` via a Google ad call/phone asset is measured by Ads call reporting/Google forwarding number, **not by adding `gtag` to booking pages**. `click_to_call` on website is at most a diagnostic click, not confirmed answered phone call or new patient. Do not remove useful first-party source attribution or turn on sensitive health-tag instrumentation merely to satisfy Ads UI.

Do not enable GA4/Google Ads tags on booking, registration, clinical form or symptom pages without clinical/privacy approval, proper consent and platform-compliant privacy engineering. Historical click identifiers are advertising attribution, not permission to share medical data.

Platform reports may be delayed/cached; verify exact current account state directly before a monetary action.
