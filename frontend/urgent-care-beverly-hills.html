Care without the hospital setting

Direct access to a physician for appropriate urgent needs

When a concern needs prompt medical evaluation but is not a life-threatening emergency, the clinic offers a direct path to physician assessment and appropriate office-based care.

  • Evaluation of appropriate acute illness and minor injury
  • Wound care and selected office procedures
  • EKG and selected laboratory testing when clinically appropriate
  • Medication and treatment decisions based on physician evaluation
  • Follow-up or referral when additional care is needed

The exact evaluation and treatment depend on the individual clinical situation. Availability of specific tests or procedures should be confirmed with the clinic.

Before you book

Urgent care is not emergency-room care

Dr. Farah VIP Urgent Care is an outpatient medical practice and is not an emergency department. If you believe you may have a life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

If you are unsure whether the clinic is appropriate for your situation, call 310-467-0101. The clinic can help determine whether an outpatient appointment is a reasonable next step, but telephone guidance does not replace emergency evaluation when one is needed.
