When travel is interrupted

Medical care without losing the day

Getting sick away from home creates a different kind of problem. You may not have a local physician, may not know which facility to choose, and may need care around business, travel or family commitments.

Dr. Farah provides physician-led evaluation for appropriate non-life-threatening urgent concerns and can help determine whether a clinic visit, approved mobile visit, virtual visit, or emergency care is the appropriate next step.

  • Acute illness and minor injury
  • Respiratory, urinary, gastrointestinal and allergy concerns when appropriate for urgent care
  • Dehydration and selected IV therapy when clinically appropriate
  • Medication-related travel questions and eligible virtual refill consultations
  • Follow-up guidance during a stay in Los Angeles

Staying at a hotel?

Call the clinic and tell us where you are staying. The office can confirm current availability, whether an approved hotel visit is appropriate, and the expected next steps.

No concierge membership fee. Visit charges, mobile-service charges and insurance responsibility depend on the service and individual circumstances.
