The physician

A doctor who treats you herself

Dr. Farah brings together years of experience in emergency and family medicine with a calm, personal, patient-focused approach. She listens carefully, explains clearly, and cares for each patient directly — the reason so many return and refer their families and friends.

Her practice combines prompt urgent care with private and mobile visits, and consultation-led rejuvenation medicine. Across all of it, the standard is the same: unhurried attention, sound clinical judgment, and honest guidance.
