What the visit may include

A clear path to surgical readiness

The exact requirements come from the surgeon or procedural team and the patient's medical history. A pre-operative visit may include:

  • Physician history and physical examination
  • Review of relevant medical history and medications
  • Medical risk assessment
  • EKG when appropriate or required
  • Laboratory testing when appropriate or required
  • Clearance documentation sent according to clinic workflow

Medical clearance is based on clinical findings. Additional testing, records, specialist input or follow-up may be required before a final determination can be made.

Before your visit

Help us prepare the right clearance

Bring the surgeon's requirements

If your surgeon or surgery center gave you a clearance form, testing list, fax number or deadline, have it available for the clinic.

Bring an up-to-date medication list

The physician may need to review medications, relevant history and recent results as part of the assessment.

Allow time for testing

A 24–48 hour target is possible when clinically appropriate and when required testing, records and results are available. It is not a guaranteed deadline.
