Medication guidelines

What may — and may not — be considered virtually

A medication appearing below does not guarantee a refill. Every request requires an appropriate clinical evaluation, verification and professional judgment.

Examples that may be considered

  • Established blood-pressure and cardiac maintenance medications such as lisinopril, amlodipine or metoprolol
  • Established asthma and allergy medications such as albuterol inhalers, fluticasone or montelukast
  • Established diabetes and thyroid medications such as metformin or levothyroxine; insulin requests require particular verification and clinical judgment
  • Established acid-reflux and digestive medications such as omeprazole or famotidine
  • Selected non-controlled urgent-care prescriptions, including antibiotics, anti-nausea medication or eye drops, only when medically indicated after evaluation

Not provided through this virtual refill service

  • ADHD stimulants such as Adderall, Vyvanse, Ritalin or Concerta
  • Benzodiazepines such as Xanax, Ativan, Klonopin or Valium
  • Controlled opioid or pain medications such as tramadol, oxycodone or hydrocodone
  • Sedative-hypnotic sleep medications such as Ambien or Lunesta
  • Controlled substances or medications that require specialist psychiatric management under clinic policy
Important: Dr. Farah VIP Urgent Care's policy is not to prescribe or refill controlled substances, Schedule II–V medications, ADHD stimulant treatments, benzodiazepines, opioid pain medications, or medications requiring ongoing specialist psychiatric management through this virtual refill service. Federal telemedicine rules may permit some controlled-substance prescribing under specific conditions, but this clinic applies a more restrictive service policy. Prescribing always remains subject to applicable federal and state law, the patient's physical location, licensure, pharmacy rules and clinical judgment.

Location matters

Telehealth follows the patient

Telehealth rules depend on where the patient is physically located during the visit. Virtual care is offered only when Dr. Farah is legally authorized to provide medical care to a patient in that location.

For patients located in California, telehealth care is held to the same standard of care as in-person medicine and requires appropriate consent, privacy and clinical evaluation.

Before you book

Be ready to state the state where you are physically located, your preferred pharmacy, the medication name and dose, and how the existing prescription can be verified securely during the clinical workflow.

The public booking form intentionally does not accept detailed medical history or medication-document uploads.
